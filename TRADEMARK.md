# The Pocket XR name and icon

The **code** of Pocket XR is free software under the GPLv3. You may use, change and share it under that licence.

The **name "Pocket XR" and the Pocket XR icon** are not part of that licence. They belong to Mike Haye and identify the
official project and its releases, so people know what they are installing.

## You may

- Say your work is "based on Pocket XR" or "a fork of Pocket XR", and link to this project.
- Use the name to talk about Pocket XR: in articles, videos, reviews, tutorials and comparisons.
- Build Pocket XR from source for yourself, unchanged, under its own name.

## Please don't, without permission

- Publish a changed version (a fork) as "Pocket XR", or with the Pocket XR icon. Give your fork its own name and icon.
- Use the name or icon in a way that suggests the project endorses you or your product.
- Use the name in another app's name or in a domain name.

To ask, write to connect@mikehaye.com. Most reasonable requests get a yes.

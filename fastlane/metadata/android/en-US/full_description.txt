Pocket XR turns an Android phone and a pair of XREAL glasses into a multi-window workspace. Your apps float around you as windows you can move, resize, curve and push back. Head tracking keeps them in place, and the phone becomes the controller.

<b>Windows in space</b>
<ul>
<li>Any app opens as a window around you, at its own size and distance.</li>
<li>Drag windows by their edges, snap them side by side, curve them, push them further or pull them closer.</li>
<li>Move, push, pull or resize every window at once.</li>
<li>Arrange layouts and presets that reopen a set of windows where they were.</li>
</ul>

<b>Seeing it</b>
<ul>
<li>3D side by side, with each window at a real depth.</li>
<li>Head tracking from the glasses' own motion sensor, paced to their display.</li>
</ul>

<b>Controls</b>
<ul>
<li>The phone as a trackpad, with taps, two-finger scroll and pinch, and three-finger swipes.</li>
<li>The Spatial Mouse: point the phone like a laser.</li>
<li>Hand tracking on glasses with a camera (XREAL Aura).</li>
</ul>

<b>What it needs</b>
<ul>
<li>A phone whose USB-C port carries video (DisplayPort alt mode), on Android 14 or later.</li>
<li>XREAL glasses: the Aura and the One are tested; the One Pro and 1S are in community testing.</li>
<li>Shizuku, to create a window for each app.</li>
<li>Optional: the Pocket XR add-on, for the cursor outside the Space and the Notifications page. It holds the accessibility service and notification access, so Pocket XR itself needs neither.</li>
</ul>
